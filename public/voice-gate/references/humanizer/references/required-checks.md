# Editorial review

Read the applicable profile before editing. Run the checks relevant to the passage. Keep review silent unless the user asks for findings.

## Meaning and source

Compare claims, attribution, quantities, dates, uncertainty, conditions, causality, negation, and obligation strength with the source. Keep exact syntax, quotations, and technical identifiers intact. Check citations against the associated claim. A more specific sentence is worse if its detail was invented.

## Reader and inherited voice

Does the passage serve the reader's task while retaining supported expression? Use the current brief or sample first, then the approved profile. A technical section can use purposeful structure without losing its voice. Do not transfer chat abbreviations, command shorthand, or formatting rates into another genre.

Check the information hierarchy. Does the main capability, change, decision, or consequence receive attention before minor settings and metadata? Keep an optional detail when it changes what the reader can do or expect; omit an absence that has no practical effect. Follow exhaustive coverage when the requested form requires it.

## Specificity and coherence

Find the claim, action, distinction, or relationship each passage contributes. Supply an example only when supported and useful. A reusable instruction can be useful without a project-specific name or number.

Keep related thoughts together when splitting makes them choppy. Inspect repeated framing when it replaces substance. Do not change useful parallel structure or punctuation merely because it occurs several times.

## Stance and development

Preserve the author's supported view and reasons. If a missing view blocks the task, ask narrowly or retain neutrality. Do not manufacture a reaction, concession, memory, aside, hesitation, or surprising detail to make a piece feel human.

Technical explanation can move directly from conditions through mechanism to consequences. Reference sections can remain independent lookup topics. Change an outline only when it impedes the task and restructuring is in scope.

## Over-correction and completion

Remove invented personality, forced slang, typos, manufactured stakes, fake contrarianism, and decorative precision. Check formulaic report scaffolding, stock conclusions, and unsolicited closing offers. End when the requested thought or action is complete.

Lint and detector findings identify passages to inspect. A numeric score or surface feature cannot establish quality, authorship, truth, or voice fidelity. Prefer no change when the edit has no clear benefit.
